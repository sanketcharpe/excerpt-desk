    Sync with Google Drive
    1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in. Your Drive appears as a drive on the PC (often G:\My Drive).
    2. Create a folder there, for example My Drive\Excerpt Desk.
    3. Press Choose folder on this PC above and select that folder.
    4. Every matter, PDF, worksheet and export saved here then syncs to Drive by itself.
